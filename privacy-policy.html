Privacy Policy

TypeSprint is designed to work without collecting personal information.

Last updated: October 2026. This page describes the default behavior of the static TypeSprint project. The site owner should review it with current legal and advertising requirements before publishing.

What the app stores

When available, your browser's localStorage stores theme preference, preferred test settings, saved test results, personal-best values and daily challenge progress. This information stays on the device and browser profile where it was created.

What the app does not send

The typing engine does not upload your typed characters or local history to TypeSprint servers. The app does not require an account, password, name or email address. It does fetch the public passage JSON file needed to run the site.

Clearing data

You can clear history or reset all TypeSprint data from the practice page. Clearing browser storage, using private browsing or changing browsers can also remove or hide saved progress. Progress does not automatically synchronize between devices.

Contact and future services

The included contact form is a static demonstration and does not transmit messages. Before launch, the owner must configure a real contact method and update this policy to describe it. If analytics, advertising or third-party embeds are added later, the owner must review consent, disclosure and retention requirements and update this page.

Children and sensitive information

Do not enter sensitive information into custom passages or contact fields. The site is a general educational tool, not a service for collecting sensitive data.

Questions about this policy should use the configured contact method on the Contact page.
