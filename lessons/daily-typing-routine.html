Lesson 08

A daily typing routine you can actually repeat.

A good routine removes the decision of what to practise next. Keep the structure simple enough to use on an ordinary day.

The 10-minute template

  1. Warm up — 2 minutes: common words at a relaxed pace.
  2. Focus — 4 minutes: punctuation, numbers, code or the error pattern from yesterday.
  3. Transfer — 3 minutes: a normal sentence passage without stopping after each error.
  4. Reflect — 1 minute: note one improvement and one next step.

Keep the target small

Do not make every session responsible for a new personal best. A useful target might be fewer corrections in punctuation practice or a more comfortable posture during a one-minute test.

Use the daily challenge as an anchor

TypeSprint's local challenge is selected from the current date. It can give the routine a consistent closing passage without claiming a public ranking.

Exercise: make a default plan

Write down your preferred duration, one practice category and the time of day you are most likely to return. Save the plan somewhere visible, then use practice mode to start it without a countdown.

FAQ

What if I miss a day?

Resume without trying to make up for it. A routine is a path back in, not a streak that needs protecting.

Can a routine be shorter?

Yes. Even one focused passage can make the next session easier to begin.
