Why TypeSprint exists

A quieter place to get better at typing.

TypeSprint is built for learners and professionals who want clear feedback, useful practice and no account wall between them and the keyboard.

Focused by default

The typing interface should make the next keystroke easy to see. That is why TypeSprint uses a restrained visual system, readable monospace text and feedback that distinguishes correction from failure.

Private by design

The test runs in your browser. Results and preferences are stored locally when your browser permits it. TypeSprint does not need your name, email, typed text or performance history to work.

Useful outside the test

A number alone is not a lesson. The lesson library explains WPM, accuracy, posture, finger placement and daily routines, while practice mode lets you work without a countdown.

Honest boundaries

There is no verified global leaderboard, cross-device sync or promise of AdSense approval. Any future advertising must be clearly labelled, configured by the owner and kept away from the typing controls.
